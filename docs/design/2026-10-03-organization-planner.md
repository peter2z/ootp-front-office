## 1. Context and goals

**The user's words.** "Optimizing my roster management at the organizational level: the best assignments for prospect development, team success and depth, positional reassignment, pitching staff and bullpen assignment and recommendations for outright release or trade due to lack of upside and roster utility. We need to be able to recognize service time limits in the lower levels (recommendations for international complex promotions) to prevent invalid rosters. Ideally at least 28-30 players per level to cover injuries and fatigue. Take both historical production and recent production into account along with scouting overall potential. Consider player timelines or potential positional changes when considering a prospect's 'path' and what kind of utility they can provide to the organization overall."

**The three decisions (2026-10-03).**
1. The 28-30 target applies to the four full-season clubs (AAA, AA, High-A, Single-A). The ACL and the two DSL clubs keep larger complex rosters with their own editable targets.
2. Per-level pro service time limits ship as OOTP's standard table, editable, with the save's observed ceilings as the defaults (rookie complex 3, DSL 4, Single-A 4, High-A 5, none at AA/AAA).
3. Decision state, minimal: accept or dismiss per move, stored per save, checked after the next import; dismissed moves stop being re-recommended. This closes item A1 (action cards) from the 2026-10-02 review.

4. **Band ceiling (decided 2026-10-03): minimum hard, maximum soft at 35.** The user wrote "at least 28-30", so the minimum is the hard side of the band and the maximum is soft: a club over its maximum gets a warning on its level card, and only `surplus`-class men leave a club for size alone; a `depth` or `prospect` man is never pushed down or out to meet the maximum. The default full-season maximum ships as 35, not 30. The reason is in the data: the 32 orgs carry 135-178 full-season men each (median 154; org 15 carries 142, below the median), and league-wide list-1 rosters run AAA 28-37-49, AA 31-38-53 and A 32-38-45 (min-median-max), so a hard ceiling of 30 would have the planner push 22 men out of the user's four clubs and leave them thinner than every AI club in the league. `targets.fullSeason.max` stays a setting, so the ceiling can be lowered later without a code change.

**What "done" looks like.** A new page, Org Planner, that for the chosen org shows each level with its roster today, its planned roster, whether the size band and the depth structure are met, and its pitching staff as the engine classes it (the rotation in order and the bullpen with the closer first); a single ordered list of moves, each naming the player, from → to, the reasons as sentences, the OOTP screen and steps, the deadline and what the next import will check; a release-or-trade list ranked so the top is actionable; a dated deadlines strip; Accept / Dismiss per move, remembered per save and verified on import; an "Org moves" chip on the dashboard; a Planner block in Settings for the size bands, the caps and the IC rules; and the Rule 5 bug in the 40-man page fixed with the planner and the page reading one function. That is 0.42.0. Per-player paths on the player card, the read-only chat tool, position and role moves, `chooseFielders` manning and the save's own rung-step calibration follow in 0.42.1 (§11); 0.42.0 sizes and classes the pitching staff (SP/RP minimums, a derived closer per rung, the rotation and bullpen order printed per level) and 0.42.1 recommends the role changes. Deterministic, under a second for 324 men, cached per org until the next import, read-only toward OOTP, shipped with release notes. Honest about what the export cannot support (§2.3, §13).

## 3. Concepts

**Rung.** One step of the ladder: `mlb, aaa, aa, high-a, single-a, complex, dsl, ic`. Built by `rungs(orgId)` from `orgTeams()` (`server/org.ts:83`, to be exported) joined to `leagues.reputation`, ordered by reputation descending; clubs sharing a reputation share a rung (the two DSL clubs); the IC pool is appended last. Labels come from the save (`ACL Dodgers`, `DSL Dodgers Mega`), so an FCL org gets the same `complex` key with its own label. A save without `leagues.reputation` falls back to `teams.level` then `league_id`, merges what it cannot tell apart, and says so in `warnings[]`.

**Eligibility.** Per player per rung, for this season and next: may he be rostered there under the hard rules (§4)? A boolean plus the reason that fails. Computed before any scoring; nothing scored overrides it.

**Forced move.** A move a rule requires regardless of merit: past a cap now, last eligible season at a capped rung, IC man approaching 20, 60-day IL to free a 40-man place. Dated, placed first, pinned during balancing.

**Readiness.** `fit(player, rung)`, a signed number blending scouting against the rung's median, production translated to the rung, and age against the rung's median (§5.4). Shown on the page as a verdict too: `ready` / `hold` / `overmatched` / `frozen`.

**Production index (P).** One number, 100 = average for the rung it is quoted at: wRC+ for bats, the mean of ERA+ and FIP+ for arms, over up to three seasons, each line against its own league-season, translated one rung at a time, regressed to 100 on 150 PA / 40 IP (§5.1).

**Utility class.** What jobs the man can hold, from revealed positions and pitching ratings: `C`, `IF`, `OF`, `super` (IF and OF), `bat-only` (1B/DH), `everyday` (one spot); `SP`, `swing`, `RP`, `CL`, with a `specialist` tag (§5.5, §5.6). This is the brief's "utility to the organization".

**Asset class.** The release/trade classification: `core | prospect | depth | surplus`, with modifiers `on40-last-option`, `on40-out-of-options`, `rule5-exposed`, `minor-fa-after-season` (§5.7).

**Path.** Per player: ceiling tier, utility class, best position (with or without a change), pitching role (with or without a change), readiness, a season-by-season rung timeline to the ceiling rung, ETA, the season he is last eligible at his rung, and every deadline that touches him (§7.2).

**Depth structure.** What a rung needs to play a season: catchers, infield with shortstop cover, outfield with centre-field cover, starters, relievers, each with a minimum and a soft maximum, inside the size band. Sizes are counted on the full roster (list 1): a hurt man holds a place and that is what "28-30 to cover injuries" means. Minimums are counted on healthy men (list 2): an injured catcher does not catch. The level card prints both numbers. The size band's minimum is hard and its maximum is soft (§1, fourth decision; §4).

**Card (move).** One recommendation: `kind`, `horizon` (`now` | `offseason`), player, from → to, reasons as sentences, OOTP screen and steps, deadline, verify predicate, decision state. Kinds: `forced, callup, senddown, promote, demote, cover, assign, position, role, protect, il60, trade, release, hold`. `hold` keeps a move a rule refused visible with its refusal ("out of options", "40-man is full"); `cover` is a promotion whose only reason is an injured man above, with his return date. `position` and `role` ship in 0.42.1.

## 5. Scoring

### 5.1 Production index (P)

- **Seasons.** Current year `y` = `MAX(year)` of the stats tables (2028), plus `y−1`, `y−2`. One line per (season, league), `split_id = 1`, `league_id <> 0`, grouped by `(player_id, year, league_id, level_id)`.
- **Index per line, at its own level.** Bats: `wrcPlus` from `computeBatting(line, leagueBaseline(league_id, year, level_id))` (`server/stats.ts:125`, `:54`). Arms: `0.5 × eraPlus + 0.5 × fipPlus`, with `fipPlus = 100 × lgERA × pf / fip` added beside `eraPlus` in `computePitching` (`stats.ts:179-211`). Capped to [0, 250].
- **Level comparison.** Each line is translated to the rung it is being judged at, one step at a time: `idx' = idx + Σ step(r → r+1)` going up (steps are negative), the reverse going down. **In 0.42.0 every step is OOTP's own factor**, `100 × (mle(from) / mle(to) − 1)` from `leagues.ml_equivalencies_avg` (AAA→MLB −15, AA→AAA −5.9, High-A→AA −3.8, Single-A→High-A −2.6, Complex→Single-A −20, DSL→Complex −8.3), and `method.rungStep` reports `source: 'mle'`. **From 0.42.1** `rungSteps()` calibrates the steps on the save itself: for every man with ≥ 200 PA (≥ 40 IP) in consecutive seasons at adjacent rungs across the last five seasons, the median change in his league-relative index; a step with fewer than 40 pairs keeps the MLE factor. It is computed once, in `runImport()` after `takeSnapshot()`, persisted in history.db (`rung_steps`, keyed by `save_name` and the game date), and read back by `computePlan`; when no row exists for the current save and date (a fixture, or a database imported before 0.42.1) the MLE table is used and `warnings[]` says so, so no request ever pays the calibration. On this save (plain OPS+/ERA+ proxy; the engine uses its own index) the calibration is worth doing: bats Single-A→High-A −5.5 (296 pairs), High-A→AA −6.8 (376), AA→AAA −5.4 (311), AAA→MLB −10.5 (160), Complex→Single-A −9.3 (64), DSL→Complex −13.6 (29 → MLE fallback); arms −10.7 (280), −10.4 (336), −10.7 (306), **−21.7** (159), −16.6 (63), DSL→Complex 23 pairs → fallback. `method.rungStep` reports each step, its source (`save` | `mle`) and its pair count.
- **Weights.** `w = recency × sampleShare`; recency 1.0 / 0.6 / 0.3 for `y / y−1 / y−2`; `sampleShare = min(PA, 450) / 450` for bats, `min(IP, 120) / 120` for SP-class arms, `min(IP, 60) / 60` for the rest.
- **Regression.** `P = (Σ w·idx' + 100·π) / (Σ w + π)`, `π = 1/3` (150 PA at full recency, the `productionScore` prior, `server/lineup.ts:511`).
- **Gates.** `P` is readable only when the raw three-season window holds ≥ 150 PA or ≥ 40 IP; below that P is null and the card says "no readable sample (NN PA over three seasons)". A sentence about *this season at this level* needs ≥ 60 PA / ≥ 15 IP (the Prospects floors, `org.ts:850`, `:901`). A `demote` needs ≥ 100 PA / ≥ 30 IP at the current rung plus the form gate. Readiness on production alone needs `P ≥ 115` with the window ≥ 250 PA / 60 IP, or this season ≥ 100 PA / 20 IP at ≥ 120.

### 5.2 Scouting (S)

- `OA`, `POT` exact from `composites()` (`org.ts:191`): `players_value.oa / pot`, then the rounded columns, then the component average.
- `z_S = (OA − medOA[rung][group]) / scaleGrade(5)`, group ∈ pos / sp / rp (`groupOf`, `server/trade.ts:42`, exported). Medians from `rungBaselines()` (cached per import; a rung with < 30 men in a group borrows the nearest rung's and says so in `method`).
- Upside `U = POT − OA` through `onScale()` (`org.ts:395`); `NEAR_CEILING = 5`, `HIGH_UPSIDE = 15` (`org.ts:398-400`).
- Ceiling tier from POT on 20-80: **regular** ≥ 50 ("MLB regular" / "MLB rotation"), **bench** 45-49 ("MLB bench" / "MLB bullpen"), **depth** 40-44 ("AAA depth, up and down"), **filler** < 40 ("organisational depth"). Anchored where the MLB population sits (median 47, 25th percentile 44 = `REPLACEMENT_PCT`, `trade.ts:57`). No aging formula on POT: the age enters through `z_A` and the asset class.
- Talent percentile (`mlbPercentiler().talentPct`, `valuation.ts:574`) is quoted for `regular`-tier men only, as context, with the caveat at `valuation.ts:555-566`.

### 5.3 Age-for-level (A)

`z_A = clamp((medAge[rung] − age) / 2, −2, +2)`, medians from `rungBaselines()` (list 2). Young for the rung ≥ +0.75 (1.5 years); old ≤ −1.

### 5.4 Fit, verdicts and thresholds

- `z_P = clamp((P_r − 100) / 15, −3, +3)`, `P_r` translated to rung `r`.
- `fit(p, r) = 0.5·z_S + 0.3·z_P + 0.2·z_A` when P is readable; `0.7·z_S + 0.3·z_A` otherwise; missing S → `0.6·z_P + 0.4·z_A`; missing both → `unscored` (listed, never moved).
- **Promote candidate**: eligible above, `fit(above) ≥ 0`, form not `poor` with a meaningful sample. Strong (sorted first, moved now even when the cap only requires it by next season) at `fit ≥ 0.5`.
- **Demote candidate** (`overmatched`): `fit(current) ≤ −1.0`, `z_A ≤ 0`, demote sample met, form not `good`, an eligible rung below exists (never from the lowest club, as `overmatched()` at `org.ts:972`).
- **Team success at the big club.** Among men of equal fit, the call-up and `protect` orderings put first the man whose utility class covers a position `rosterHoles(orgId)` (`valuation.ts:700`) names as the MLB club's thinnest; the card says "the big club is thinnest at SS" (the thinnest position on this save today). `org.mlbThinnest` carries the list the page prints. As built, protections go by the user's decision (accepted, then open, then dismissed), then fit, then this, then the earlier deadline, then `player_id`, and POT plays no part (§14); call-ups keep the general tie-break.
- Everything else is `hold`, and a hold with a story (a poor season, a last option year, a cap next year) gets a card with sentences and no move (§5.8, Zazueta).
- Positional fit and role do not enter `fit`; they make their own move kinds (0.42.1).

### 5.5 Positional fit and position changes

- Positions considered: those the game has revealed (`fielding_rating_posN > 0`, the `gloves()` rule). A man "covers" N at current ≥ `scaleGrade(40)`.
- `bestPosition = argmax overall_<pos>` over considered positions (OOTP's own per-position value), ties by potential then current.
- A `position` move (same rung; 0.42.1) when all hold: `bestPosition ≠ listed`; `overall_best ≥ 1.05 × overall_listed`; `pot_best ≥ scaleGrade(50)` or `pot_best ≥ pot_listed + scaleGrade(10)`; moving up the spectrum (toward C, SS, CF: order 2, 6, 8, 5, 4, 9, 7, 3, `FIELD_POSITIONS`, `lineup.ts:177`) also needs current ≥ `scaleGrade(45)` at the target; catcher is never proposed for a non-catcher. No crowd test: a man whose card says so is recommended wherever he is (the Gonzalez case fires in the DSL: 2B 55/60 vs SS 30/50, OOTP 495 vs 443, `fielding_experience4` 188 at 2B against 82 at SS and 29 at 3B).
- A `cover-position` note (not a move) when a rung is short of SS or CF cover and a man covers it at ≥ 40: "list him at SS as well" — quoted on his promote card (0.42.0, since the structure check already finds the shortfall).
- Timing: `now` at AA and above; "plan it for his {next rung} season" below. Sentences quote both ratings, both OOTP values and the experience counter (0-200 scale, column = position number).
- Utility class from covered positions (current ≥ 40, or current > 0 with potential ≥ 50): `C` at 2; `IF` at ≥ 2 of {4, 5, 6}; `OF` at ≥ 2 of {7, 8, 9}; `super` when IF and OF; `bat-only` when only 3 and/or 10; `everyday` otherwise.

### 5.6 Pitching roles

From `players_pitching`, never from `players.role` (reported as "listed as"):
- `pitches` = count of the twelve `pitching_ratings_pitches_*` ≥ `scaleGrade(40)`.
- **SP** = stamina ≥ `scaleGrade(45)` and `pitches ≥ 3`. On this save 141 of 161 active MLB starters (88%) pass; 152 (94%) pass at stamina 40; 145 of 224 role-12 relievers (65%; 68% with closers) have stamina ≤ 35; 65 relievers (29%) are SP-class and get "SP-class, listed RP".
- **Swing** = SP-class with stamina 45-49, or stamina ≥ 45 with exactly 2 pitches. As built, this is the one cell the ratio tie-break decides: a man in it is classed SP or RP by which of the org's medians his `overall_sp / overall_rp` ratio sits nearer, and stays swing only when there is no lean (§14).
- **RP** otherwise. **CL** per rung = the healthy RP with the highest `stuff + 0.5 × hold` among those within `scaleGrade(5)` OA of the best reliever; `ROLE_CLOSER = 13` (moved to `valuation.ts` beside `ROLE_STARTER`) breaks ties.
- **Specialist** tag when `|vsl_stuff − vsr_stuff| ≥ scaleGrade(10)`.
- **Staff per level (0.42.0).** From the classes and fits the engine already holds, each level card prints its staff as a sort, no new scoring: the rotation = SP-class men on the planned roster by fit, five at AA and above, six below (a `swing` man fills a short rotation and is tagged); the bullpen = the derived CL first, then the remaining RP- and swing-class men by fit, each specialist tagged "vs L" / "vs R". This is the "pitching staff and bullpen assignment" of the request as far as the classes carry it; the role *moves* follow in 0.42.1 and the fatigue-aware bullpen notes in 0.43.
- `overall_sp / overall_rp` is a tie-break only (every pitcher's SP value exceeds his RP value because it bakes in innings; MLB-active medians 1.54 SP / 1.56 RP / 1.42 CL). A `role` move (same rung, `to.role`; 0.42.1) when the derived class differs from `players.role` and he is healthy; RP → SP also needs stamina ≥ 50, three pitches ≥ 45 and a rotation short at his rung (fewer than five SP-class arms at AA+, six below); CL changes at AA and above only.

### 5.7 Release / trade classification (asset class)

After fit, on 20-80 through `scaleGrade`:
- `core`: POT ≥ 55, or OA ≥ 50 and age ≤ 30.
- `prospect`: POT ≥ 45 and `U ≥ 8` and `z_A ≥ 0`.
- `depth`: OA ≥ medOA[rung][group] − 3, or SP/CL-class arm at AA+, unless `surplus` by age below.
- `surplus`: none of the above; also any man with `age ≥ medAge[rung] + 6` and `POT ≤ medOA[rung]` (no growth and old for the level), and `age ≥ medAge + 3` at the Complex/DSL (the brief's 25-27-year-olds at the ACL).
- Depth value: a man keeps a place whatever his class when his removal breaks a structure minimum; the card says "kept for structure: third catcher at Tulsa".

Modifiers → kind:
- `surplus` + minor contract + not on 40 → `release` (horizon now, no deadline; the sentence says what he blocks).
- `surplus` or `depth` + on 40 + last option year or out of options → `trade`, deadline = trade deadline (2028-8-3) if ahead, else Rule 5 date.
- `depth` + minor-FA after the season → "minor-league free agent after this season: re-sign only if the level still needs him" (no move).
- `prospect` / `core` + Rule 5 eligible + protect gate → `protect`.
- `noTrade` or `core` never reaches the trade list; IC men never reach either list.
- Trade vs release for a `surplus` man not on the 40: `trade` when `overall_value ≥ valueAt(mlbPools[group], REPLACEMENT_PCT)` (`trade.ts:107`, `:130`, exported) or money is owed (`yearsAfterThis > 0`); else `release`.

## 6. Algorithm

Entry: `computePlan(orgId): Plan` in `server/planner.ts`, pure after the loads, cached per org.

1. **Rungs.** `rungs(orgId)` from `orgTeams()` + `leagues.reputation`; attach `targets` and `serviceCaps` from Settings by rung key; MLB size from the MLB league row.
2. **Load** (one query each): `orgPlayers()` (`org.ts:138`, exported, with `bats, throws, date_of_birth, league_id` added under `hasColumns`), `team_roster` lists, roster status (columns guarded by `tableColumns`, the `rosterCrunch` pattern at `rosterops.ts:75-80`), `contractsByPlayer()`, `valuesByPlayer()` + the per-position values, fielding rows, pitching rows, `rosterCrunch(orgId)`, `rosterHoles(orgId)`, `rungBaselines()`, the rung steps (the MLE table in 0.42.0; the persisted `rung_steps` row from 0.42.1), the three-season lines (one grouped query per table for org players), the league-season baselines for the org's leagues (one `GROUP BY league_id, year` per table over the three seasons, fed into `baselineCache` so `leagueBaseline()` hits), `seasonFormByPlayer` per club, the calendar.
3. **Profile every man** → `Path`: rung, health (list 2 / list 4 for minors, `healthOf()` for the 26), on26 / on40 / il60, eligibility for all rungs this season and next, P, S, A, `fit` at current ± 1 and MLB, utility class, role class, ceiling tier, asset class, option state, Rule 5, minor-FA, deadlines. IL men `frozen`; no grades → `unscored`.
4. **Forced moves**, in order, each dated: past the cap → promote now; last eligible season → `forced` (horizon by fit; to the first eligible rung above, which may be two steps up, and then only at `fit ≥ 0`, else the release/trade list); IC at 19 / birthday within a year → `assign`; IC pool at capacity → the low-priority `assign`; 40 full with an IL man ≥ 60 days → `il60`; out of options on the 26 → flag. Pinned at their destinations.
5. **MLB 26.** As it stands. `correspondingMoves()` promote verdicts become `callup` + `senddown` pairs when the displaced man passes `optionState` and the called man has a 40-man place or `fortyManRoom().comesOff` names one; otherwise `hold` with the refusal. Form gates apply. Ties among candidates go to the man who covers an `mlbThinnest` position (§5.4). (0.43 rebuilds the 26 from paths.)
6. **Target rung per man** (non-forced, non-frozen): above when a promote candidate, below when overmatched, else current — provisional until balancing. An injured man above with no healthy replacement of his group makes the best eligible man of that group below a `cover` candidate.
7. **Balance**, top-down AAA → AA → High-A → Single-A → Complex → DSL, then one bottom-up pass, at most three rounds (terminates). Per rung: (a) **cover the field first** — in 0.42.0 each of the eight positions must have a healthy man on the planned roster who covers it (≥ 40), and a position nobody covers is filled before any group is counted; in 0.42.1 this becomes `chooseFielders(candidates, FIELD_POSITIONS)` (`lineup.ts:258`, with `FIELD_POSITIONS`, `AVERAGE_GLOVE`, `DEF_POINTS_PER_RATING` exported) over `Candidate[]` built from the men targeted here (fit as `off`, revealed gloves as `defense`, both rescaled to the value range `slotValue` expects); (b) fill each group to its minimum by fit within the group; (c) `planned = list-1 stays + arrivals − departures`; **under `min` (hard)** → pull from the rung below by `fit(this rung)` among eligible men, a short group first, men whose cap expires this season first; still short → `need` note; **over `max` (soft)** → `warn` on the card, and push out only `surplus`-class men, lowest fit first (never `core`, `prospect` or `depth` for size alone; never a man whose removal breaks a minimum; never into a rung he is ineligible for), to the rung below; a `surplus` man eligible nowhere below joins the release/trade list; the card still says "41, 6 over your 35" when surplus men alone do not bring it inside. A push into a rung that is itself over `max` cascades one step in the same round. The DSL pair: a man stays on his current club when it has room, else the emptier club. The IC only ever loses men.
8. **Staff and notes on the final rosters**: the rotation and bullpen per level (§5.6, a sort); `cover-position` notes attached to promote cards. In 0.42.1, position and role moves (§5.5, §5.6), at most one per man.
9. **Release / trade list** (§5.7) from men who ended under no band, were pushed out as `surplus`, or are classed `surplus`, ranked by fit ascending within class, so the top is actionable.
10. **Sentences, steps, deadlines, verify predicates** per card; the rule or threshold that fired is the first sentence by construction, so no card has an empty `reasons`.
11. **Decision overlay** per request: dismissed keys removed (kept under `show=dismissed`), accepted keys marked with their outcome; counts after the overlay.

**Tie-breaks everywhere**: `fit desc, covers an mlbThinnest position first, POT desc, OA desc, age asc, player_id asc`; every SQL ends `ORDER BY player_id`; `chooseFielders` receives candidates in that order; no `Math.random`, no wall clock (the game date is `leagueGameDate()`). Same export + same settings → byte-identical JSON, pinned by a test. As built, two orderings differ: protections go by fit, then the big club's thinnest position, then the earlier deadline, then `player_id`; the release-or-trade loop walks the lowest fit first, then the older man, then `player_id` (§14).

**Cost** (324 men), measured on this save where it could be: 9 SQL reads (~50 ms), `rosterCrunch` once, `correspondingMoves` once (~100 ms; the dashboard already pays it), `rosterHoles` once, the baselines for ≤ 7 leagues × 3 seasons — through `leagueBaseline()` one league-season at a time this measured ~97 ms each, 2 s for 21, because the career tables have no `(league_id, year)` index; so the importer adds `idx_<table>_league_year ON (league_id, year)` to both career tables (the `importer.ts:234` pattern), after which each aggregate is an index range and the 21 are expected under 50 ms (to be measured and recorded in the release), and the single `GROUP BY` of step 2 is the fallback if they are not — 324 × 4 fit evaluations, the eight-position cover check 7 × 8 × ≤ 45, balancing ≤ 3 × 8 × sort(≤ 60). `rungSteps()` is not in the request path at all: its calibration (7,316 batting lines at ≥ 200 PA over five seasons, 93 league-season baselines, measured 2.4 s as one `GROUP BY` per table and 9.0 s pair by pair) runs once per import in 0.42.1. Budget: cold < 400 ms with the index, warm < 1 ms; the synthetic-org test asserts < 1,000 ms, and the measured cold figure for org 15 goes in the 0.42.0 notes.

**Caching.** `planCache: Map<orgId, Plan>`, `rungBaselineCache`, `rungStepCache`; all dropped by `clearPlanCache()`, called from `clearImportCaches()` (`server/api.ts:157`) and from `PUT /planner-settings`. The engine itself reads the decisions it acts on while it computes: an accepted or dismissed 40-man question, a dismissed call-up, and a dismissed trade or release of a 40-man man feed the 40-man count, and only while no import has settled them (outcome null or not-yet). So every write to the decisions drops the org's cached plan (`onPlanDecisionsChanged`: accept, dismiss, reopen, a retired dismissal, and a verifier stamp, which drops every org's), and each cached plan also carries a fingerprint of the decisions it was computed under (`decisionsRead`), so a decision written around the store is caught too. The overlay of decisions on the cached plan is still read per request (one SELECT, and one more for the fingerprint).

- Helpers, exported (the `watchPlayer` pattern, `history.ts:403`): `decidePlanMove(orgId, move, decision)` (upsert; the server looks the move up by key in the current plan, so the client never writes a predicate), `reopenPlanMove(orgId, key)`, `planDecisions(orgId)`, `verifyPlanDecisions()`.
- **Dismissed** keys are removed from `moves` and counted; shown under `show=dismissed` with a Restore button. A dismissal holds until the planner stops producing that key (he moved, the destination or kind changed), when it is marked `moot` and no longer shown. As built, a dismissal of a move with a deadline also lapses once that deadline's window has closed, and the move is asked again (§14).
- **Accepted** keys stay in `moves` as `decision.state = 'accepted'` with the outcome; once `done` they are folded under `show=accepted` and counted in `counts.done`.
- **Verification** runs in `runImport()` (`api.ts:168`) right after `takeSnapshot()`: for every accepted row with `outcome IS NULL OR outcome = 'not-yet'`, evaluate `verify_json` against the new league.db: predicate holds → `done`; the man is still at `from_team_id` (or still in the org for release/trade) → `not-yet`; somewhere else → `changed` ("accepted, but he went to Tulsa rather than Oklahoma City"); no longer in the org for a non-release kind → `moot`. Stamped with the new `leagueGameDate()`. `not-yet` rows are re-checked on every import until `deadline_date` has passed, then marked `changed`. The brief's simple check, did his level change, is reported alongside as `levelChanged` from the last two `rating_snapshots`. The page prints what was seen, never what happened: "Accepted 2028-5-15 · not yet seen in the 2028-8-4 export" / "Done — on Tulsa in the 2028-8-4 export". As built, the verifier also reads every dismissed row that has a deadline and no outcome, and stamps it `moot` once the window has closed.
- **Rung steps (0.42.1)**, same file pattern: `rung_steps (save_name, game_date, side, from_rung, to_rung, step, source, pairs, PRIMARY KEY (save_name, game_date, side, from_rung, to_rung))`, written by `rungSteps()` in `runImport()` after `takeSnapshot()`, read by `computePlan` for the current save and date, MLE fallback when absent.

## 13. Risks and open questions

- **The size band's ceiling.** Decided (§1, decision 4): min hard / max soft with the full-season maximum at 35. Should the user later want a hard 30, `targets.fullSeason.max` goes to 30 and the "only surplus leaves for size" line in §6 step 7 widens to `surplus → depth`; the balance tests in §11 are written against the setting, not the number, so they stand either way.
- **Service caps are not exported.** The defaults are OOTP's standard table and match this save's observed ceilings; a league with custom limits will plan against wrong caps until the user edits them. Every cap move quotes the cap it used and links to Settings; the level card says "cap: default — check League Settings" until a value is saved. Whether OOTP counts a season at 172 days or at season end is unverified. The two-steps-up rule for the DSL men assumes OOTP applies the cap on arrival; if it applies it only at season's end, those three men could pass through Single-A for a year, and the setting lets the user cap the DSL at 3 to make the planner say so.
- **OOTP screen names** (`OOTP_STEPS`) describe OOTP 27 menus from memory ("Transactions → Assign to …", "Add to 40-Man Roster", "Trading Block"); check once in the game and correct in the one table.
- **IC destination.** OOTP sends a 20-year-old to "your lowest minor league team"; whether it permits IC → ACL directly is unverified, so the verify predicate for `assign` accepts any rostered club.
- **Verification is an inference**, as `contractChanges()` warns (`history.ts:46`): a released man re-signed reads `done`; a call-up and a sale look alike until the predicate is read. The card says what was seen.
- **OOTP's own minor-league AI** may re-shuffle rosters when the user has not taken manual control of an affiliate; the export does not say which clubs the user controls, so `not-yet` / `changed` may describe moves the user never made. Open question: a per-level "OOTP controls this club" toggle in Settings.
- **Rung steps and the fallback.** 0.42.0 translates by OOTP's AVG equivalency alone, a crude proxy for a run-creation index that overstates the complex steps (−20 Complex→Single-A against −9.3 measured) and understates the jump into the majors (−15 against −21.7 for arms); `method.rungStep` says so. 0.42.1 calibrates on the save's own consecutive-season pairs at import time; the complex steps have few pairs (29 and 23 on this save) and keep the MLE factor. Sentences always quote the raw lines, so a wrong step cannot invent a fact.
- **The baseline cost.** `leagueBaseline()` measured ~97 ms per league-season on this save because the career tables carry no `(league_id, year)` index; the planner's budget depends on the index the importer adds in 0.42.0, which only exists after a re-import. A database imported before 0.42.0 falls back to the grouped load of §6 step 2 (about 1.2 s per table cold, once per import) and the server log says "re-import to index the stats tables".
- **Early-season samples.** In April almost every production term is gated off and the plan leans on scouting and age; the DSL has no 2028 lines until June and the ACL opened on 5-8. The MethodNote says so and `method.gates` makes it visible.
- **The first plan is still large, but not thin.** Tulsa at 41 with 19 relievers and 64 men at a cap: the first run lists dozens of offseason moves and a release list headed by the surplus men, but with the maximum soft it does not push depth or prospects out of the full-season clubs, and the user's 142 stays near the league's 154 median rather than falling to 120. The org line, the horizon fold and the ranked release list are there so the user acts on the top and ignores the rest; whether to hide offseason moves until a user-set date rather than August 1 is an open question for the first review of the page.
- **Rule 5 counts.** The crunch page's `counts.issues` counts the protect-gated men only, so the chip stays readable; the eligibility fact lives on every row as a flag, so the page's 115 and the chip's 3 are two different things by design and the header says both.
- **Option semantics** (`options_used` incremented the day a man is optioned, `options_used_this_year` as the flag) are read off Petersen and Zazueta; a save that counts differently would mislabel last option years. `optionState` is one function with a test matrix, so the fix is one place.
- **`healthOf()` for minor leaguers** cannot be trusted (`is_active` is 0 for all of them); the planner reads list 2 / list 4. A minor-league club whose export lacks `team_roster` loses the IL distinction and the plan says so in `warnings[]`.
- **`chooseFielders` scaling** (0.42.1). `slotValue` was tuned for OOTP value totals in the hundreds with `DEF_POINTS_PER_RATING = 8`; the planner rescales fit onto that range before the call so the glove does not dominate. A test pins the rescale.
- **Position values from OOTP** (`overall_<pos>`) embed OOTP's view of bat and glove together and refresh on OOTP's own schedule; the 5 per cent margin, revealed positions only and the sentence quoting both ratings keep the recommendation conservative.
- **Scale conversion** is proportional (`scaleGrade`); medians are per save so `z_S` is immune, but the asset-class cut-offs (45/50/55) and the ceiling tiers convert proportionally, known to run high on 1-10 / 1-20 saves.
- **Two DSL clubs as one rung**: keeping each man on his club is deterministic but may split a shortstop pair; 0.43 balances per club.
- **The big-club tie-break** reads `rosterHoles()`, which judges the 26 by OOTP value at each position; it orders ties only, so a worse-fit catcher never jumps a better-fit shortstop, and it says nothing when the 26 has no hole.
- **Open questions for the user**: the size-band ceiling above; should `release` ever be recommended for a man under 21; should the planner respect OOTP's trading-block flag (`trade_status = 2`) as a trade already in motion and limit `trade` to men `blockedIds()` does not list; should accepting a `release` also offer "put him on the trading block first" as the OOTP step; and should the Rule 5 protect gate's production bar (110) be a setting.

## 14. Implementation notes (2026-10-03, as built)

The 0.42.0 scope above was built the same day. Where the code departs from the text above, the code is right and this section says how:

- **Where a pinned man is counted.** A forced move with an `offseason` horizon pins the man where he stands: the planned roster figures count men where they are today plus the moves dated `now`. (Relocating offseason moves made Tulsa's planned figure read 59.)
- **Promotion on merit never breaks a minimum.** A promote candidate whose departure would leave his club under its size minimum or under a structure minimum gets a `hold` card instead ("Ready for Triple-A at a fit of 1.1, but Tulsa would be left with 1 catcher against the 2 the level needs without him, and a promotion on merit never breaks a minimum."). Without this both DSL clubs were drained below 30.
- **Starter class at the complex, DSL and IC rungs** uses stamina alone; the three-pitch requirement applies from Single-A up (no DSL arm in this save has a third rated pitch).
- **Rule 5 "worth a place" on the 40-man page** is judged on grade alone (`rule5ProtectGate` with no production index), so the page says 1 worth a place on org 15 where the planner, which has the production index and the minor-league free-agent rule, lists 6 protections (two minor-league free agents, four Rule 5 men) and two 40-man holds for the full 40-man, as of the third fix pass. The page counts every eligible man (115) and lists them in `rule5Eligible[]`.
- **`PlanRosterRow.flags: string[]`** carries facts that are never moves (out of options on the 26). Men designated for assignment or on waivers stay frozen on their club's rows with the fact in `flags` and appear in `leaving[]`.
- **An over-maximum note** is printed in the level's `needs` ("37 on the planned roster, 2 over the 35 you set; only surplus men are moved for size.").
- **Thin baselines** borrow the median from an adjacent rung only, never from the far end of the ladder.
- **The High-A → AA MLE step** is −3.7 (`Math.round(−3.75)`), not −3.8.
- **Verification** reads where a man stands from `team_roster` rows (list 1, 2 or 4) before `players.team_id`, so an export that moved only `players.team_id` reads `not-yet`. An accepted move the engine no longer produces is rebuilt from its stored row and listed under `show=accepted` and `show=all` with its outcome, never under `open`, and `counts.done` counts it. A `done` or `moot` decision is retired: if the engine produces the same key again it shows as open, with "Recommended again after it was done on {date}", and the dashboard chip counts it. `show=open` means `decision.state === 'open'` only.
- **Where the import found him.** `plan_decisions` carries `seen_team_id` / `seen_label`, added to an older table by `ALTER TABLE`. The verifier stamps the club in the same words the plan's moves use ("Tulsa Drillers", "International complex"), or "out of the organization", and nothing while the move is `not-yet`; the page prints "Done — on Tulsa Drillers in the 2028-8-4 export".
- **When an offseason move is judged.** An accepted `offseason` move stays `not-yet` until the first import on or after the next season's Opening Day (`leagues.start_date`); `done` and `moot` are stamped as soon as they are seen. The "still on his old club → not-yet, elsewhere → changed" rule applies only to `now` moves between rungs; a protection, a 60-day list, position, role or rostered move reads `not-yet` wherever he plays until its deadline.
- **A save switch** shuts decisions until the new save's import finishes: accept and reopen answer 409, and nothing is verified or retired.
- **The bullpen** lists the CL and the RP- and swing-class men; an SP-class arm with no rotation spot is in neither list.
- **The two DSL clubs are judged one club at a time.** Each structure row on the DSL card is the club with the fewest men in that group, against one club's need, and its note gives each club's count; `PlanLevel.clubs` carries each club's size against the per-club band, and needs name their club. `now.groups` and `planned.groups` stay the two clubs together, so the card prints no "Today" structure line under the DSL. A pull never takes a man from a club that would fall under a size or group minimum unless the rung below can refill the place, and nothing refills the DSL, so a shortfall the DSL cannot spare becomes a need one rung up.
- **The moves table** uses `SortableTh` on Kind, Player, Age and Deadline; `ColumnPicker` is bound to stat groups and was not used.
- **`src/api.ts` mirrors `server/planTypes.ts` by hand**; the browser build never reaches into `server/`.
- **The import ring** `history.ts → dashboard.ts → planner.ts → plandecisions.ts → history.ts` was broken by importing `DATE_KEY` from `db.ts` in `history.ts`.
- **Cost.** Before the performance pass, the first plan for org 15 after an import measured 5.3 s cold (4.0 s on later cold calls, 0 ms warm): eight `seasonFormByPlayer()` calls at ~310 ms each of bare `MAX(year)` scans, plus one more inside `correspondingMoves()`, plus two `organization_id` scans in `rosterCrunch()`. The pass memoises the latest stats year (`latestStatsYear()` in `org.ts`, cleared with the org cache), computes the org's season form in one pass (`seasonFormByClubs()` in `form.ts`), and has `buildIndexes()` create whatever index is missing, at import and at boot, so an older database gains `year` on the two career tables, `organization_id` on players and the `(league_id, year)` composites without a re-import. Measured afterwards on org 15, three fresh processes each: first plan 517-529 ms (builder's own runs 480-600 ms), second cold call after `clearPlanCache()` 128-136 ms, warm under 1 ms; `GET /api/dashboard/15` first visit about 0.9 s (was 6.1-6.4 s), later visits about 240 ms, and the plan is computed once for the chip and served from cache to the page. The plan JSON is byte-identical before and after (321,907 bytes, same hash). What remains of the cold 500 ms: `ratingScaleMax()` in `valuation.ts` (four unindexed `MAX(rating)` scans, about 180 ms, paid once per import by whichever page runs first), `rungBaselines()` about 65 ms, `contractsByPlayer()` about 55 ms, the league baselines about 100 ms across some 70 range queries, and about 70 ms of planner JavaScript. - **Dismissed keys leave the lists.** While a dismissal is not retired, its move is removed from `deadlines` and from `releaseOrTrade` (re-ranked from 1) under every show filter.
- **Done and moot decisions are retired.** A retired decision no longer applies: the same key produced again is an open move ending "Recommended again after it was done on {date}: he is back where the move started." (for a dismissal retired because the plan stopped listing it, "Recommended again: it was dismissed on {date}, and the plan stopped listing it by the {date} export."; since the third pass, for a dismissal whose deadline has passed, "Asked again: it was dismissed on {date} for the {deadline} deadline, which had passed by the {date} export."), and the dashboard chip counts it.
- **`show=open` means open only.** Accepted and dismissed moves are never listed under it; the page fetches `show=all` and filters itself.
- **`seenAt` in the Done and Changed sentences.** `MoveDecision.seenAt` is copied from `seen_label` and is null on open moves. The card reads "Done — on Tulsa Drillers in the {date} export", "Changed — on X, not Y", or "Not done by the {date} deadline — still on X" when the import found him on the club the move started from. The engine hands the store its own club label ("International complex" for the complex), so the comparison with `from.label` holds. Since the second pass a move about a list (a protection, a 40-man hold, a 60-day move) is never "Changed": it reads "Not done by the {date} deadline — on X, not the 40-man roster, in the {date} export", and since the third the date is the one stored with the decision (`MoveDecision.deadlineDate`), not the live card's.
- **The chip opens on `kind=decision`.** The dashboard's Org moves chip opens `#/planner?kind=decision` with the default open filter: the seven kinds that ask for a call (forced, call-up, protect, demote, trade, release, hold), so the page lists exactly as many moves as the chip counted.

### The second and third fix passes

Two more passes followed on 2026-10-03, each answering a regression review of the pass before it. Org 15 figures are still from the 2028-5-15 export. After the third pass the plan holds 122 moves (forced 58, six of them dated now; protect 6, promote 14, demote 2, assign 1, trade 1, release 7, hold 33, cover 0), 54 of them folded under offseason, 8 release-or-trade rows and 105 deadlines.

**The 40-man ledger.** The second pass put the 40-man count in one ledger inside `buildPlan`, and everything that states or changes the count goes through it. `peekPlace` says where the count stands for one more addition without taking anything. `claimPlace` takes an open place, else the next man who can cheaply come off, else reports the 40-man full, and records the claim by man; `releasePlace` gives a claim back, so its place opens again or the man who was to come off goes back to the head of the line. `freePlace` counts the place the plan's own trade or release of a 40-man man frees when the move is dated before the Rule 5 draft and was not dismissed, and takes him out of the come-off line, since he is going anyway. Only a protection counts a freed place, because a call-up is made today, before any trade, and the protection's sentence names the move: "The 40-man would hold 34 of 40, counting the place the trade of Michael Petersen frees, so a place is open".

**Protections by fit.** Protections are ordered by fit at the big club, best first (§5.4), then the man who covers the big club's thinnest position, then the earlier deadline (a minor-league free agent's season end, else the Rule 5 date), then `player_id`. The first pass's rule that minor-league free agents go first was dropped, since it let a man who walks earlier jump a better one. The protection loop now runs after the release-or-trade list, so a man the plan trades or releases is skipped before he claims a place. The cards are listed in the order they claimed (`Draft.seq`), and the third pass made the rule exact: the K in "after the K protections above" is the number of protect cards listed above that card. A man's other cards no longer state his count; they point to his 40-man card instead ("His 40-man place is asked on a card of its own, linked to this one"). On org 15 that corrected Halvorsen's and Morales's promotions, which are listed below all six protections; Halvorsen's had said "would hold 34 of 40".

**Decisions feed the count.** The plan reads the org's decisions while it computes (`decisionOn`). A 40-man question the user accepted claims its place before the fit ordering, and stays a protection when no place can be found ("The 40-man is full at 40 of 40, so a place has to be cleared for him"); since the third pass it still counts among the protections above the cards after it. One the user dismissed claims nothing, so its place goes to the men after it, but its card is still made, its clause a peek at the count, so the dismissal keeps a live key. A dismissed trade or release of a 40-man man frees no place. Each man's 40-man question has one key, shared by the protection and by the 40-man hold that stands in for it while no place is open. The second pass keyed it `protect:<id>:<rung>:40man`; the third dropped the rung, leaving `protect:<id>:40man`, because carrying out a promotion the plan itself recommended (Morales: promote and protect) orphaned the accepted protection and brought its card back open. `migratePlanDecisions` rewrites stored keys of the old shape once, and keeps the latest decision when one man has two. The 40-man hold carries the protection's `on40` check, so accepting it is verified the same way, and it keeps its deadline through the fold, which the first pass had lost: the Rule 5 date, or a minor-league free agent's season end (Harlan and Gauthier on org 15).

**Lapsed decisions.** From the third pass a decision steers the engine only while its outcome is null or `not-yet` (`decisionLive`). `done`, `moot` and `changed` are settled, so an acceptance an import found not done no longer reserves a place every later winter. A dismissal of a move with a deadline lapses once its window has closed: the verifier, which before read only accepted rows, stamps it `moot`, the engine ignores it, and the card comes back open, ending "Asked again: it was dismissed on {date} for the {deadline} deadline, which had passed by the {date} export." A dismissal with no deadline stays until its key disappears, as before.

**The cache.** Because the plan reads decisions, every decision write drops the org's cached plan through `onPlanDecisionsChanged` (accept, dismiss, reopen and a retired dismissal; a verifier stamp drops every org's). Each cached plan also carries a fingerprint of the live decisions it was computed under (`decisionsRead`: 40-man questions, call-ups, trades and releases), so a decision written around the store, by hand or by a test, cannot leave a stale count behind (§6).

**A dismissed call-up.** The second pass gave a dismissed call-up's place back to the protections after it. The third stopped placing the man at the big club at all: he stays where he is, no send-down or 60-day move is made for him, and the count is only peeked, while the call-up card is still made so the dismissal keeps its key. If he is Rule 5 eligible and passes the gate, he gets his 40-man question like anyone else, linked to the call-up card.

**Refills and promises.** A pull, a trade fill or a merit promotion takes a man only if his club can lose him. In the second pass a man was free to go (`freeToGo`) when his leaving broke no group or size minimum at his club; otherwise `refillsFor` looked below for a man to refill every group his leaving would break, preferring one who fills them all, and `spare` recorded the refills as promised, so no other pull could count on them. A trade then needed a fill who was free to go, and a trade with none became a hold that says so first ("{club} cannot cover his place from below: he is the second catcher there, trading him would leave one against the two the level needs, and every catcher below who could come up would leave his own club short."). The third pass found that a promise did not hold: `owed()` asked whether the refilled club was still short with the men owed away from it counted as present, so a second pull from the same club could take the promised man again. Now `owed()` counts the men a club is itself to send up as gone, and repeats until nothing more lapses; a promise lapses only once another man brings that club back to its minimum. `freeFor(m, club)` replaced `freeToGo` and the second pass's `freeNow`: an owed man may go only to the club he is owed to, and any other man must be free with every owed man counted as gone. The merit promotion and the trade fill ask it directly. The field, group and size pulls and the need notes ask `canSpare(m, club)`, which applies the same rule to an owed man and otherwise looks for refills with every owed man counted as gone, and both demotion steps skip an owed man and count the men owed away as gone. `refillsFor` never offers an owed man, so `spare` never writes over a live promise. The "cannot cover" sentence says which reason turned the men below away, including one "promised to refill another club already". Org 15 has no pull that needs a refill, so none of this shows there.

**The first eligible rung above, and the cap holds.** A man the cap closes the next rung to now steps up to the first rung above that admits him this season and next (`stepUpOf`), never the big club, and the pulls, merit promotions, trade fills and need notes see a man further down whose rungs in between are all closed to him, when his fit is 0 or better. A promotion past a closed rung says so: "The Complex (capped at 3) is closed to him: he will have 4 pro service years by Opening Day 2029, so Single-A is the first level above the DSL that admits him." On org 15 that is Aquino, who takes the Single-A place. A man the cap passes over a level and who has no other card gets a cap hold that names the caps first and then why he waits: his fit there under 0, a poor season, a minimum at his own club, the club above at its maximum, or next in line. The third pass made it name the need above before the minimum that holds him (Arvelo and Urena: "…; Ontario Tower Buzzers is short of infielders: five against the six the level needs, but Los Angeles (DSL) Dodgers Mega would be left with six infielders against the eight the level needs without him, so he moves up when it can spare him."), and say when the level that admits him closes: for a 3-year DSL man this is the last season Single-A can take him, so the nine holds whose fit there is under 0 end "…next year he is at the DSL cap, 4 of 4 pro service years, and must open 2030 at High-A or above, or leave." The holds for a man refused for room, or kept by a minimum at his club, likewise open with the need above when his club cannot spare him (Manley, Geraldo and Victorino on org 15), and the need is read on every club of the rung above (`lacksAbove`). Forced-move sentences count the steps ("one step up", "two steps up").

**Today's class beside the planned one.** Each pitcher keeps `roleClassToday`, set once where he is classed at his club today, beside `roleClass`, the class at his planned rung. A level's Today counts read today's class, and so does his row at the club he leaves; every other row shows the planned class. A release card counts the men at the released man's club in the class each plays there in the plan, which is the count the structure check let him go on. The third pass weighed counting them by today's class and kept the planned one, since the sentence would otherwise disagree with the plan.

**The swing cell and the SP/RP ratio.** The ratio now decides the one cell the matrix leaves undecided: stamina from 45 up to the starter's 50 with the pitches, or stamina of 45 or more with exactly two pitches. A man there is SP or RP by which of the org's medians his `overall_sp / overall_rp` sits nearer (`ratioLean`, the medians taken over men outside the cell), and he stays swing only with no lean or a figure missing; the SP and RP cells are never touched. A raw comparison was not used, since every pitcher's SP value exceeds his RP value (§2.2) and every man in the cell would have become a starter. The brief's wider band (stamina 45-54 either side of the cut, or exactly three pitches) turned decisive starters into relievers on org 15, Blake Snell and Roki Sasaki among them, so it was narrowed to the cell. On org 15, 29 pitchers changed class, every one a swing man: 21 became starters and 8 relievers. No swing man is left, and the Complex's Today line reads SP 12 / RP 13.

**Each sentence once.** `renderMove` adds every reason through one `say()`, which splits the text into sentences (`sentencesOf`) and drops any the card has already said, wherever it first said it: the lead, the standard production, scouting, Rule 5 and options sentences, the tail, the cover note and the contract sentence. A sentence ends at a full stop and a space before a capital, including after a level or a position code ("Double-A.", "2B."), but never inside a figure ("0.86"), after an initial ("A. J. Smith", "Luis C. Gonzalez"), or after "St.", "Jr." and the like; every name and club label in the org with a stop in it is kept whole (`dottedNames`). A lone "C." is not a sentence end, against the brief's wording, because it has the shape of a middle initial (1,562 names in the save carry a stop) and no card reason ends on the catcher's code. On org 15 no card repeats a sentence.

**Release order by fit, and which hold leads.** The release-or-trade loop walks the men lowest fit at their own rung first, then the older man, then `player_id` (`releaseOrder`), where before it walked by `player_id`. So where a club held to its minimum can let only some of its surplus go, the lowest-fit go. On org 15 Almora is released and Outman kept for structure at Oklahoma City, and Feliz and Noriega are kept for size at DSL Mega, which stands at its minimum of 30 once Aquino goes up.
